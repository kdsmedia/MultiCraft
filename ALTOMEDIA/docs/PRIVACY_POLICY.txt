This Privacy Policy describes how Altomedia Indonesia ("we", "us", "our", or
"the Developer") collects, uses, discloses, and protects the information of
users ("you" or "User") of the MultiCraft mobile application ("the App",
"MultiCraft", or "the Service"), available for Android on the Google Play
Store. The App is published under the package identifier
com.altomedia.multicraft.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge that
you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy and the processing
of your information as described herein. If you do not agree with this Privacy
Policy, please do not download, install, or use the App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and to handling your personal
information in a transparent, secure, and lawful manner in accordance with
applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the California Consumer Privacy
Act (CCPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the
Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Information You Provide Directly
    The App does NOT require you to create a user account, register, or log in
    with a username and password. We deliberately designed the App so that no
    personal account credentials are collected or stored on our servers.
    You can play MultiCraft immediately after installation without providing
    any personal information.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically
    When you install and use the App, the following categories of information
    may be collected automatically through the App or through third-party SDKs
    integrated into the App:

    (a) Device and technical information: device model, manufacturer, operating
        system version (Android version), screen resolution, device language,
        timezone, and a device identifier used solely for the purposes
        described in this policy.
    (b) Usage and diagnostic information: crash reports, performance data,
        log data, and app stability metrics that help us identify bugs and
        improve the App.
    (c) Google Play Games services data: when you choose to interact with
        Google Play Games features (such as achievements, leaderboards, or
        saved games), Google may process your gameplay data in accordance
        with Google's own privacy policy. The App requests the
        play-services-games and play-services-auth APIs to enable these
        features.
    (d) Google Play Services and Firebase data: the App integrates Google
        Play Services and Firebase. These may collect device identifiers,
        usage data, and analytics. The Firebase project is registered as
        "altomedia-indonesia" (project number 739146490998).
    (e) Update-check information: the App checks for updates by downloading
        a small version file (ver.txt) from the GitHub repository
        (raw.githubusercontent.com/kdsmedia/MultiCraft). This request is
        unauthenticated and does not send personal data.

1.3 Storage permissions and local files
    The App stores game data (worlds, settings, downloaded assets, texture
    packs, and mods) in the app-private external storage directory
    (Context.getExternalFilesDir). This directory is sandboxed to the App and
    is not accessible to other apps without explicit user action. The App does
    NOT request broad storage permissions (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) on
    Android 10+ because it uses scoped, app-specific storage. On uninstall,
    this data is removed by the system.

1.4 Permissions requested by the App
    The App requests the following Android permissions:
    - INTERNET — for downloading game assets and checking for updates.
    - ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — optional, used by certain game mods if enabled.
    - POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — to show unzip/asset extraction
      progress notifications.
    - FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC — to run the asset
      extraction (unzip) foreground service so long operations are not killed.
    - INSTALL_SHORTCUT (launcher) — to optionally add a shortcut to the home
      screen.
    The App does NOT request access to contacts, SMS, call logs, camera,
    microphone, precise location, body sensors, or other sensitive
    permissions.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
    (a) To operate, maintain, and provide the App and its features;
    (b) To check for and notify you of available updates;
    (c) To download and extract game assets, texture packs, and mods that
        you select;
    (d) To enable Google Play Games features (achievements, leaderboards)
        when you choose to use them;
    (e) To monitor and improve app stability, diagnose crashes, and fix
        technical issues;
    (f) To analyze aggregate, anonymous usage trends to improve gameplay
        and user experience;
    (g) To comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

We do NOT use your information to build a profile of you for advertising
purposes by the Developer. The App itself does not display ads.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The App is a sandbox-style building and exploration game suitable for general
audiences, including children. We do NOT knowingly collect personal
information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age
in the relevant jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent.

The App does not require registration. Google Play Games data is processed by
Google under its own terms; parents may manage Google Play Games through the
child's Google account settings.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with
personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us so we can
take appropriate action.
